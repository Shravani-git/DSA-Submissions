# [Product of Two Numbers](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/product-of-two-numbers/1)
## Easy
You are given two integers x and y, your task is to return the product of x and y.
Examples :
Input: x = 5, y = 2Output: 10Explanation: Product of 5 and 2 is 10.
Input: x = 100, y = 5Output: 500Explanation: Product of 100 and 5 is 500.
Constraints:-104 &lt;= x, y &lt;= 104
